Why a Sump Pump Fails

Most failures are predictable.

Stuck float

The float switch jams and the pump never starts or never stops.

Power outage

Storms that fill the pit often also knock out power.

Failed check valve

Water flows back into the pit after each cycle.

Age and burnout

Old pumps and motors wear out, especially when they run often.

Installing or Replacing a Sump Pump

A new pump is a short job when the pit is sound.

1

Inspect the pit

The plumber checks the pit size, the float and the discharge line.

2

Set the pump

The pump goes on a stable base with a check valve on the discharge.

3

Connect the discharge

The discharge line is routed away from the foundation.

4

Test

Water is poured into the pit to confirm the pump cycles.

What 2023 Showed About Sump Pumps

ABC7 Chicago reported that two Calumet City residents had sump pumps and check valves but still took sewer water in July 2023, when heavy rain overwhelmed aging sewers. South Holland's mayor said a small number of homes had basement flooding from sump pump failures in the September 2023 storm.

A pump helps with groundwater and storm water, but it does not stop sewer backup. Ask about a backwater valve if backup is your concern.
