What Installation Work Includes

Installation is more than hooking up a fixture. It includes the supply lines, shutoff valves, drain and vent connections and a pressure test before anything is covered.

The plumber checks existing lines for capacity, since adding fixtures to a small supply can lower pressure elsewhere in the house.

Installing Yourself or Hiring a Plumber

Some tasks are manageable at home and some are not.

A homeowner can often handle

  • Swapping a showerhead or aerator
  • Replacing a faucet with matching connections
  • Changing a simple supply hose

Call a licensed plumber for

  • Moving or adding drain and vent lines
  • Gas, water heater and sewer connections
  • Anything behind a wall or under a slab

Planning Ahead Saves Money

Decide on fixtures before the plumber visits. Rough-in locations are set early, and changing a fixture later can mean opening a finished wall.

Ask what the job needs from you, such as clear access, shutoff times and a place to store materials. A little preparation shortens the visit and keeps the price close to the clear price you were given.
