Emergency Hydro Jetting in Lansing, IL

Emergency hydro jetting in Lansing, IL uses a high-pressure water jet to scour grease, scale, sludge and root fibers from the inside of a drain or sewer line. A licensed plumber threads a hose with a special nozzle through a cleanout, then pulls it back while the water cuts the buildup loose. Call our 24/7 line when a cable has not been enough.

A jet cleans the whole pipe wall, where a cable only punches a hole through the blockage. That is why it suits lines that keep clogging, and why we usually confirm the pipe's condition with a camera first.

When a Backup Calls for a Jet

If a main line has backed up more than once, or a cable cleared the water but it slowed again within weeks, the pipe wall is probably coated. A jet removes that coating so the pipe carries its full width again.

If sewage is coming up in a basement right now, stop using water in the house and call the 24/7 line. We book same-day appointments when available, and the plumber will decide whether to cable first or go straight to jetting.

Cable or Jet: Which Tool Gets Used

Both tools have a place. The plumber picks based on the line, not on the invoice.

A cable works well for

  • A single clog in a sink, tub or toilet branch
  • A fresh stoppage with a clear obstruction
  • Short runs with tight bends

A jet works well for

  • Grease and sludge lining a kitchen or main drain
  • Roots that have grown into joints. See sewer line cleaning
  • Lines that clog again every few months

How a Hydro Jetting Visit Runs

Jetting is controlled work, and the plumber checks the pipe before turning up the pressure.

1

Camera first

A quick sewer camera inspection shows the pipe material and any breaks.

2

Pick the nozzle

Different nozzles cut roots, scour grease or push debris forward.

3

Jet in passes

The plumber works from the cleanout downstream, in passes, to avoid pushing debris into a worse spot.

4

Verify with the camera

A second camera pass shows the pipe wall and confirms flow.

Pipes That Do and Do Not Suit Jetting

Jetting is safe on sound pipe but wrong for a pipe that is already failing.

Pipe conditionJetting?Why
Sound cast iron, clay or plastic with buildupUsually yesThe jet clears buildup without stressing the pipe
Cracked, collapsed or offset pipeNo, repair firstPressure can worsen a break. See sewer line repair
Heavy roots in clay jointsYes, with the right nozzleRoots are cut and flushed out, though they can regrow
Unknown conditionCamera firstThe camera decides if jetting is safe

Why Lansing Basements Care About a Clear Main

The Little Calumet River forms Lansing's northern edge, and a restoration plan for the levee describes about 1.25 miles of levee inside the village. After heavy storms, a partly blocked main line makes basement backups more likely.

That is one reason homes near the river and the older streets around Ridge Road benefit from clearing the main before the wet season rather than after the first backup.
