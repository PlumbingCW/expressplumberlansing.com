Frozen Pipe Repair in Lansing, IL

A frozen pipe is a water line with ice inside it, which blocks flow and can split the pipe as the ice expands. If you turn on a faucet and only a trickle comes out on a cold day, open the tap, warm the pipe slowly and call a plumber if you see a crack or leak.

This guide explains how to thaw a pipe safely, what not to do and how to prevent freezing. It is written for homeowners in Lansing, IL.

Pipe Frozen or Cracked: Immediate Steps

Open the faucet served by the frozen pipe so melting water has a place to go. Find the main water valve and know how to close it in case the pipe cracks.

If you see water leaking, close the main valve at once and call the 24/7 line. A frozen pipe may already be split, and the leak can start as it thaws.

How to Thaw a Pipe Safely

Slow and gentle is the rule.

1

Open the tap

A faucet open on the frozen line lets water escape as it melts.

2

Apply gentle heat

Use a hair dryer or warm towels, starting nearest the faucet and moving back.

3

Never use open flame

A torch can ignite framing and damage pipe and soldered joints.

4

Check for leaks

Watch the pipe as it thaws, and shut the main if you see water.

If You Are Away During a Freeze

A burst pipe in an empty house can run for days. Before you travel in winter, keep the heat on, ask a neighbor to check the house and consider shutting off the main water valve and draining the lines.

A smart water sensor near the water heater or the main can alert you to a leak while you are away. If you come home to a wet floor, shut the main and call our 24/7 line.

Preventing Frozen Pipes

Insulate pipes in unheated spaces with foam sleeves, and seal drafts near pipes with caulk or foam. Keep the heat on and set to a reasonable temperature even if you are away.

During a hard freeze, let a faucet drip on the coldest line, and open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls. These small steps keep warm air moving around the pipes.

After the Thaw: Check for Hidden Damage

A pipe that froze may have a small crack that only leaks when water flows. After thawing, turn the water on and check the pipe, the ceiling and the floor below for moisture over the next day.

If you see a stain or hear dripping inside a wall, call for leak detection before the damage spreads. Homes in nearby towns such as Thornton and Lynwood face the same risk.
