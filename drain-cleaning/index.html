Water Backing Up Right Now? Start Here

Stop using every fixture that drains to the same line. A flush, a shower or a load of laundry sends more water into a pipe that has nowhere to put it, and it usually comes out at the lowest opening in the house.

If water is rising through a basement floor drain or a toilet, keep children and pets away and call the 24/7 line. Backed-up drain water can carry bacteria, so wear rubber gloves and boots if you have to touch anything wet.

Skip the chemical drain opener once a line is already full. It sits in the standing water, can burn skin on contact and makes the job harder and riskier for the plumber who opens the line afterward.

Drain Problems We Clear in Lansing Homes

Most calls start with one slow fixture and end with a full stop. These are the blockages we see most often, and each one calls for a slightly different tool.

  • ✓
    Kitchen sinksGrease, coffee grounds and food scraps cool inside the pipe and build a gray plug that slows the sink a little more each week.
  • ✓
    Bathroom sinks and tubsHair and soap scum knot together just past the stopper or in the trap, which is why a tub can drain slowly for months before it quits.
  • ✓
    Toilets that will not clearA toilet that gurgles or rises when another fixture runs usually points to a blockage past the toilet itself, so the fix may sit in the branch line.
  • ✓
    Floor and laundry drainsLint, sludge and sediment settle in basement drains. A backup here often means the main line is partly blocked, as covered under sewer line cleaning.
  • ✓
    Main drain linesWhen every drain in the house is slow, the blockage sits downstream of all of them, often at roots, scale or a sagging section of pipe.

Which Method Fits Which Clog

The right tool depends on where the blockage sits and what it is made of. This table shows how a plumber usually reads the symptoms.

What you seeWhat it usually meansTypical method
One slow sink or tubHair or grease near the trapHand auger or small cable
Several fixtures slow at onceBlockage in a branch or the mainCable machine from a cleanout
Floor drain backs up when the washer runsMain line restricted downstreamCable first, then hydro jetting to scour the walls
Same clog returns every few monthsRoots, scale or a low spot in the pipeSewer camera inspection to find the cause

A camera look costs a little more up front and often saves repeat visits, because it shows whether the pipe needs cleaning or repair.

What to Do Before We Arrive for a Clogged Drain

A few safe steps keep a clog from turning into a mess.

  • ✓
    Stop using waterEvery flush or load of laundry sends more water toward the blockage.
  • ✓
    Do not pour drain cleanerStrong chemicals can burn skin and eyes, and they can pool in a blocked pipe. Tell the plumber if you already used some.
  • ✓
    Keep children and pets away from standing waterBacked-up water can carry bacteria, so avoid touching it and wash hands after any contact.
  • ✓
    Clear the areaMove rugs and stored items away from the floor drain so the plumber can reach it.

Why Older Lansing Homes Clog More Often

A 2025 water affordability study of the Village of Lansing reports that 39 percent of local homes were built in 1959 or earlier. Lansing grew fast after the war, and the Lansing Journal notes a building boom in the late 1940s and early 1950s for returning veterans and young families.

Pipes from that era can include cast iron, clay and galvanized steel, and rough pipe walls hold grease and scale more easily than modern plastic. If your home is in that group and a drain keeps slowing down, a camera inspection is the quickest way to tell a simple clog from a pipe that is wearing out.

What Affects the Price of Drain Cleaning

We do not quote a flat price over the phone because the blockage decides the work. These are the factors that move the number.

Blockage locationA clog at the trap is a short job. One forty feet down the main line takes more cable and more time.
Tool requiredA hand auger, a cable machine and a water jet each cost different amounts to run.
AccessA cleanout near the foundation saves time. Without one, the plumber may have to remove a toilet to reach the line.
Camera inspectionAdding a camera pass costs more but shows the cause and any damage, which helps you decide on repairs.
