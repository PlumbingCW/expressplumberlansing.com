Dual Sump Pump Installation in Lansing, IL

A dual sump pump system has two pumps, usually in one pit or two, so one keeps running if the other fails or if water arrives faster than one pump can move it. It is a sound choice for basements that have flooded before or sit in low ground.

This guide explains how a dual system is arranged, when it is worth it and what to plan with an installer. It is written for homeowners in Lansing, IL.

How a Dual System Works

A primary pump handles normal flow. A second pump, set slightly higher, starts if the water keeps rising or if the primary fails.

Some systems use a battery or water-powered backup as the second pump, so the basement is protected during a power outage as well. Others use two electric pumps on separate circuits.

Why Pumps Fail When You Need Them Most

The same storm that fills your pit can also knock out your power. A pump also works hardest when rain is heavy, so a worn pump is most likely to fail in the middle of a storm.

A dual setup reduces both risks. A backup that does not depend on household power keeps the pit clear during an outage, and a second pump covers the failure of the first.

Discharge Lines and Where the Water Goes

Both pumps need a discharge line that carries water away from the foundation. If the line empties near the house, the water can run back toward the wall and reenter the pit in a loop.

In winter, a discharge line can freeze at the outlet. Ask the installer about an outlet that stays open and a line that slopes away. A frozen line can defeat even two pumps.

Lansing, IL Note: Lessons From the 2023 Storms Nearby

The Lansing Journal reported that in September 2023 about six inches of rain fell in two hours in South Holland, closing streets and causing basement flooding from sump pump failures in a small number of homes. The mayor credited the Deep Tunnel project for diverting nearly 1 billion gallons of rainwater.

ABC7 Chicago reported that residents in Calumet City with sump pumps and check valves still took in sewer water in July 2023. A dual system protects against pump failure, but not sewer backup, so ask about a backwater valve too, and see sump pump installation and repair.

Maintenance for Two Pumps

Test each pump separately every few months by pouring water into the pit. The alarm should sound when the second pump runs, and you should hear both pumps start.

Clean the pit of debris once a year, check the check valves and replace backup batteries on schedule. A dual system is only as good as its weakest part.
