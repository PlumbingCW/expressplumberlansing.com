Main Line Blocked: Immediate Steps

Stop running water everywhere in the house. Toilets, tubs, sinks and appliances all share the same line.

If sewage is entering the basement, keep family and pets away from it and call the 24/7 line. Wear gloves and boots if you have to touch anything wet, and do not use a wet vacuum on sewage.

Why Roots and Older Lines Matter Here

Lansing grew from the 1893 union of Oak Glen, Bernice and Lansing, and the Lansing Journal notes that indoor plumbing arrived in 1921. Mature trees along older streets can reach into aging joints in search of water.

If your main clogs again after a cleaning, ask for a camera visit. It shows whether roots or a damaged joint are the cause, so you can fix the source rather than clear it every season.

When a Clean Is Not Enough

Cleaning restores flow, but it does not repair a broken pipe. If the plumber finds a collapse, a deep sag or a joint that keeps letting roots in, the honest recommendation is repair, not another cleaning.

We say so plainly, and we show you the camera footage when we can. That way you decide with the facts in front of you.
