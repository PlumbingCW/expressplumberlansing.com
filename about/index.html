Lansing Express Plumbing runs a 24/7 emergency plumbing line for Lansing, Illinois and the nearby Cook County towns of South Holland, Thornton, Lynwood, Glenwood and Calumet City. We send licensed and insured plumbers, and we give a clear price before work starts.

This page explains how a call works, what we stand behind and how you can check any plumber's license for yourself.

What We Do

We handle the plumbing problems that cannot wait, such as burst pipes, sewer backups, failed water heaters and flooded basements, and the planned jobs that go with them. Our plumbing services page lists everything we cover.

How to Check Any Plumber's License

The Illinois Department of Public Health licenses plumbers under the Illinois Plumbing License Law and offers an online plumbing license verification tool. We encourage you to use it for any plumber you hire, ours included.

IDPH also says plumbing contractors must register with the state and carry insurance. Ask any company about both before you agree to work.

If your problem is not on that list, call anyway. We will tell you honestly whether we can help.

Three Steps Anyone Can Take in a Plumbing Emergency

Learn where your main water shutoff is before you need it. Turning it off stops a burst pipe or a failed supply line from doing more damage while you wait for help.

Stay out of standing water if any outlet or appliance is nearby, because water and electricity do not mix. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 first.

Take photos of any damage before you clean up. They help if you later talk with your insurance company.

Not every call is an emergency. Many customers phone ahead for a water heater swap, a new fixture or a camera look at an old sewer line, and we book those visits for the next open slot.
