Business Plumbing Emergency: What to Do First

Shut off the water at the main or at the affected fixture, and move stock, paper and electronics away from the wet area. If a drain is backing up, stop using sinks and restrooms that share the line.

Tell staff which restrooms are closed and post a sign for customers. Then call the 24/7 line so we can book a plumber while you protect the space.

Keeping a Business Open During Plumbing Work

Tell the plumber when customers arrive and which restrooms and sinks you cannot do without. Many jobs can be staged so one restroom stays open while another is repaired.

For a backed-up floor drain, ask about clearing the line first and scheduling any larger repair for after hours. Photograph any wet stock or damaged finishes before cleanup, since your property insurer may ask for proof. Keep the names and numbers of your landlord and building manager handy so approvals do not slow the repair.
