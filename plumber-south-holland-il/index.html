Before We Arrive: Steps for a Plumbing Emergency in South Holland

Take these steps while you wait for a plumber. They limit damage and make the visit faster.

Shut off the main

Turn the main water valve clockwise. In many homes it sits near the meter in the basement.

Kill power to wet areas

Switch off breakers for rooms with standing water, and stay out of flooded basements with live outlets.

Stop using drains

If water is backing up, no flushing, laundry or dishwasher until the line is cleared.

Clear the way

Move items away from the work area and know where your cleanout is.

Low Ground, High Stakes: Why South Holland Basements Take On Water

Wikipedia notes that South Holland sits on low ground near the Calumet River at about 600 feet, and that Dutch settlers first called it de Laage Prairie, meaning Low Prairie. That low ground is why a sump pump is basic equipment for many homes here.

After a storm on September 17, 2023, about six inches of rain fell in two hours and several streets flooded, according to the Lansing Journal. The mayor said a small number of homes had basement flooding from sump pump failures, and he credited the Deep Tunnel project for diverting nearly 1 billion gallons of rainwater.

If your pump is old, noisy or has never been tested, check it before the next storm. Our sump pump repair page explains what we look at, and the village runs a Flood Mitigation Program created in 1994 that offers technical and financial help to residents.

Water Quality and Lead: What the Village Report Says

South Holland's 2024 water report says lead in drinking water mainly comes from service lines and home plumbing. It advises flushing the tap for 30 seconds to two minutes before drinking or cooking when water has sat for hours.

The report does not list water hardness or a service line inventory, so we do not quote either. If you want to know what your pipes are made of, ask a plumber to check where the line enters the house, and see water line repair and replacement.
