Drains and Sewer Lines

Water that will not drain is the most common reason people call us, and the right fix depends on how deep the blockage sits.

Drain cleaning

A slow sink or a floor drain that gurgles usually needs a cable or a jet, and our guide to emergency drain cleaning explains how we choose.

Hydro jetting

When grease or scale keeps coming back, hydro jetting scours the whole pipe wall instead of poking a hole through it.

Sewer line cleaning

If every drain in the house is slow, sewer line cleaning clears the main line to the street.

Sewer camera inspection

A sewer camera inspection shows what is inside the pipe before you pay for a repair.

Sewer line repair

Roots, cracks and sagging pipe call for sewer line repair, from spot fixes to liners and replacement.

Water Heaters and Heating

Hot water is a daily need, and a failed tank is an emergency in winter.

Water heater repair

Leaks at fittings, failed elements and pilots that will not stay lit are covered under water heater repair.

Water heater replacement

A tank leaking from the body is a case for water heater replacement.

Water heater installation

New builds, moves and fuel changes fit under water heater installation.

Tankless water heaters

If you want hot water on demand, read about tankless water heater service.

Boiler repair

Pressure loss, noise and no heat are handled in boiler repair.

Leaks, Pipes and Water Lines

Burst pipe repair

Leak detection

Water line repair

Repiping

Rusty water and repeated pinholes may mean it is time for repiping.

Plumbing installation

New bathrooms, laundry hookups and fixtures belong to plumbing installation.

How to Choose Where to Start

If water is flowing where it should not, start with the burst pipe or sump pump pages and shut the water off first. If sewage is coming up, stop using water and call the 24/7 line.

If the problem is slow and steady, such as a drip or a slow drain, you can take time to read. We still book same-day appointments when available, and we give a clear price before work starts.
