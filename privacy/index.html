Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Lansing Express Plumbing handles when you call us or visit this website. We keep it short and plain.

Last updated 2026-11-14.

Information You Give Us

When you call our line, we ask for your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem so we can book a plumber. We use that information to schedule and complete your service.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

This Website

This website has no contact forms, and we do not ask you to type personal details into it. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and maps from Google Maps, so Google may receive your IP address and browser details when those load.

Please read Google's own privacy policy for how it handles that information.

How We Use and Share Information

We use call information to book and complete work, to improve our service and to follow the law. We share what is needed with the plumber who visits you and with service providers who help us run our line.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your Choices and Contact

You can ask us what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@expressplumberlansing.com or calling (855) 575-2890. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page.

Coverage Area

Our Lansing, IL Service Area

Our line covers Lansing and the nearby Cook County towns of South Holland, Thornton, Lynwood, Glenwood and Calumet City. Call from anywhere in that area and we will confirm your address before we book a visit.
