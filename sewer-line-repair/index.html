Sewage in the House? Do This Now

Stop using water everywhere. Do not flush, run the dishwasher or the washer. Every drop goes to the same blocked line.

Keep people and pets out of any room with sewage on the floor. Wear gloves and boots for anything you must handle, and call the 24/7 line. Cleanup of sewage-soaked materials is best left to professionals.

Signs the Sewer Line Itself Is Damaged

A blocked drain and a broken sewer line can look alike at first.

Several drains back up together

The blockage or break sits past every branch.

Gurgling and sewage odors

Air is being pushed back through traps.

Wet or sunken spots in the yard

A leaking or collapsed pipe can undermine soil.

Repeated clogs after cleaning

The pipe may be broken, offset or full of roots, not just dirty.

What Not to Do After a Backup

Do not pour chemical cleaners into a line that is full of water, and do not keep flushing to see if it clears. Both can make the problem and the cleanup worse.

Take photos of any damage before you move things, and keep any receipts. If the blockage turns out to be a damaged pipe, a camera inspection gives you a record to share with your insurer. A first step is often drain cleaning to get water moving again before repairs are planned.

What 2023 Taught the Southland About Sewer Backups

ABC7 Chicago reported that Calumet City, just north of Lansing, was declared a disaster area in July 2023, with residents describing sewer water in finished basements even where sump pumps and check valves were installed. Heavy rain overwhelms aging sewers, and the cause is not always the pipe on your property.

That is why a camera check matters. It shows whether the problem is inside your line, which you can repair, or downstream in the public system, which the Village of Lansing handles. Ask the village before you pay for private work if the problem seems to start at the street.
