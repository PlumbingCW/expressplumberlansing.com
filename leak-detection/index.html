Active Leak? Limit the Damage First

Shut off the main water valve if water is visibly spreading, then turn off power to any wet room from the breaker. Move furniture and valuables away from the area.

Do not poke at a bulging ceiling with your hands. A bubble of trapped water can release all at once, so place a bucket below and call the 24/7 line.

Signs There Is a Hidden Leak

Not every leak shows as a puddle. These signals tell you to look closer.

A meter that moves with everything off

Shut off all water use, then watch the meter. If it still turns, water is going somewhere.

A sudden rise in your water bill

A higher bill with the same habits suggests a running toilet or a hidden line leak.

Stains, bubbling paint or musty smell

Moisture behind a wall or above a ceiling often shows as discoloration before it drips.

Hissing or low pressure

A pressurized line leaking inside a wall can hiss, and pressure may drop across the house.
