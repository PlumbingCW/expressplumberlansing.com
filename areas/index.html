Towns With Their Own Pages

Each of these places borders Lansing or sits within a short drive of the village.

South Holland

To the northwest, South Holland sits on low ground near the Calumet River, and our South Holland emergency plumber page covers basement flooding and sump pumps.

Thornton

West of Lansing, Thornton is a compact village with a famous quarry, and we explain what to expect on the Thornton emergency plumber page.

Lynwood

South of Lansing, Lynwood is a newer village founded in 1959, and the Lynwood emergency plumber page follows the seasons.

Glenwood

Southwest of Lansing, Glenwood runs along an ancient Lake Chicago shoreline, and its Glenwood emergency plumber page covers forest preserve roots and basements.

Calumet City

North of Lansing, Calumet City has had repeated floods, and the Calumet City emergency plumber page starts with sewer backups.

ZIP Codes We Cover

Lansing's own ZIP code is 60438. South Holland uses 60473, with 60426 for Harvey and Phoenix and 60419 for Dolton nearby. Thornton uses 60476, and 60430 covers nearby Homewood.

Lynwood uses 60411, and Glenwood uses 60425, with 60411 also covering Ford Heights and Chicago Heights. Calumet City uses 60409, and 60633 covers Burnham.
